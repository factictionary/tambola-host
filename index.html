Questions, answered

Frequently asked

Is Tambola & Housie Host really free?
Yes — the app is free to download and use. It contains ads, but no paid features are required to host a complete game.
Do I need an internet connection to host a game?
No. Everything runs locally on your device. No account, no sign-up, no data connection needed.
Can I use this on a TV for a large audience?
Yes. The dedicated TV / HDMI Presenter Mode lets you cast the full 1–90 board to a TV or projector so the whole room can follow along.
Which languages are supported?
17 in version 2.0, including English (NZ & UK), Te Reo Māori, Hindi, Punjabi, Marathi, Gujarati, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, French, German, Russian, and Arabic. More on the way.
How is the random number generator kept fair?
The app uses a 100% fair RNG that calls every number 1–90 exactly once per game. There are no duplicate calls and no skipped numbers — the entire room can verify it.
Does it support ticket generation too?
This version focuses on being the best caller in the room. For full ticket hosting, validation and prize tracking you can also try the developer's companion app — All-In-One Tambola & Housie.
